The Ethiopian Bible

The Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church preserves a distinctive biblical heritage often described as an 81-book canon. Book lists and arrangements vary across traditions and manuscripts.

Distinctive books

1 Enoch and Jubilees are preserved in full in Geʽez, the classical language of Ethiopian Christianity. These writings also have histories beyond Ethiopia.

Other books to explore

  • Jubilees: A retelling of events in Genesis and early Exodus organized around periods of time.
  • 1–3 Meqabyan: Ethiopian books distinct from the Greek books commonly called Maccabees. English samples are linked from the reading collection.

Understanding the canon

Scholars distinguish broader and narrower Ethiopian canonical lists. Counts, names, and groupings can vary; the familiar number 81 is a starting point for learning, not a promise that every manuscript contains the same books.

English reading collections and the canon

The GOD4.us English reading collection includes 84 entries and two companion works, 2 and 3 Enoch. It combines historical English editions and church-order writings. Some editions draw on related textual traditions.

Church-order writings

The five documented additions are Sinodos: Statutes of the Apostles (George Horner, 1904), Sinodos: Apostolic Canons (George H. Schodde, 1885), 1 Dominos: Testament of Our Lord (James Cooper and Arthur John Maclean, 1902), 2 Dominos: Epistle of the Apostles (M. R. James, 1924), and the Ethiopic Didascalia (J. M. Harden, 1920).

These are historic English editions, not new translations commissioned by GOD4.us. In particular, the Testament of Our Lord text listed here was translated from Syriac, rather than directly from Geʽez.

Text sources and editorial notes

The collection draws on older public-domain English editions. Some source books have no traditional verse numbers; any sentence-based verse divisions created for the collection are editorial and should not be treated as historical citations. The transcriptions have been spot-checked, but not fully proofread word by word.

4 Baruch is excluded from this collection.
