# KJV Textus Receptus Word Study data

The English spans come from CrossWire's KJV SWORD module 3.1, commit `d490be7e34762deb2c76cb2c1306d4808e27890d`. Its OSIS SHA-256 is `4bcbfe45722fca396adbeac9c81025bbb173cb1e30296da90864f0400cb0e3f5`. CrossWire credits the Bible Foundation, KJV2003 volunteers, and Maurice A. Robinson. CrossWire declares GPL distribution for the module and grants general public use of the tagging work. The publisher identifies Crown rights in the English base text. [Publisher notice](https://www.crosswire.org/sword/modules/ModInfo.jsp?modName=KJV) · [Pinned OSIS](https://gitlab.com/crosswire-bible-society/kjv/-/raw/d490be7e34762deb2c76cb2c1306d4808e27890d/kjv.osis.xml) · [Pinned configuration](https://gitlab.com/crosswire-bible-society/kjv/-/raw/d490be7e34762deb2c76cb2c1306d4808e27890d/kjv.conf)

The Greek witness is Maurice A. Robinson's parsed Textus Receptus, with the public-domain notice from the byztxt project at commit `33af6e9361f8db0afd353714d05c75f44e5555b9`. Byztxt credits Maurice A. Robinson and maintainer Ulrik Sandborg-Petersen. The importer converts the source's transliterated forms to Unicode Greek. It retains exact verified KJV spans, position indices, lexical identities and morphological codes. It excludes supplied words from links and leaves earlier accepted alignments and manuscript overrides intact. [Pinned Greek source and public-domain notice](https://github.com/byztxt/greektext-textus-receptus/tree/33af6e9361f8db0afd353714d05c75f44e5555b9)

Individual lexical fields are from the existing GOD4.us accepted Greek dictionary data. That data credits Strong's Greek Dictionary (1890), Ulrik Petersen XML 1.4 and the Open Scriptures snapshot. Unavailable or inconsistent entries are omitted. They do not affect the verified positions. The publication report pins the dictionary source inventory and every generated shard.
