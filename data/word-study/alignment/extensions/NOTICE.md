# Shell 85 Word Study alignment attribution

These chapter files contain derived English spans and native token positions. The Bible text and witness labels remain tied to their installed editions. The extensions are distributed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) with these source notices.

- [unfoldingWord Literal Text v92](https://git.door43.org/unfoldingWord/en_ult/src/tag/v92), the [Hebrew Bible](https://git.door43.org/unfoldingWord/hbo_uhb/src/tag/v3.0.0) and the [Greek New Testament](https://git.door43.org/unfoldingWord/el-x-koine_ugnt/src/tag/v0.34) are CC BY-SA 4.0. The importer transforms their annotations into validated span and token positions.
- The [Berean publisher interlinear tables](https://bereanbible.com/downloads.htm) are CC0 1.0. The importer verifies separate unfoldingWord and Berean positions against the installed original-language sequence before adding joint evidence.
- [CrossWire KJV OSIS](https://www.crosswire.org/sword/modules/ModInfo.jsp?modName=KJV) is pinned in the [Shell 76 source report](../../../../docs/shell-76-word-study.md). New position groups require exact English including editorial boundaries, complete native sequence ownership, and compatible morphology.
- The pinned parsed [Scrivener 1894 Textus Receptus](https://github.com/honza/textus-receptus/tree/2bac2dae4a0961c84d6d544a40cbef8b8d8c4e32) is attributed in the [Scrivener notice](../kjv-scrivener/NOTICE.md). A labeled alternate can be used for KJV when no earlier verified alignment or manuscript override is active.
- Same-wording edition projections retain edition-specific coordinates, verified English and original-token checks, and the donor edition IDs for each group. Source notices for the accepted donor evidence remain in [these data folders](..).

The [publication manifest](../../../../docs/shell-85-alignment-publication.json) records the pinned inputs and every output SHA-256 hash.
