# WEB alignment data

The original work by unfoldingWord is available from the [ULT publisher](https://www.unfoldingword.org/ult), the [Hebrew Bible publisher](https://www.unfoldingword.org/uhb), and the [Greek New Testament publisher](https://www.unfoldingword.org/ugnt). Copyright © 2022 by unfoldingWord, as stated in the pinned sources. The generated WEB alignment index and chapter JSON files are available under [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

GOD4.us used ULT alignment milestones and the ordered UHB and UGNT witnesses to build these mappings. The importer discarded incompatible and ambiguous records, converted source positions to GOD4.us token indexes, and replaced source English offsets with exact WEB Reader character offsets. The chapter JSON files contain selected WEB verses, the app’s original-language token identities, and links between them. This data is a GOD4.us adaptation. The English Scripture is the public-domain [World English Bible Protestant Edition](https://ebible.org/engwebp/).

UHB incorporates the [Open Scriptures Hebrew Bible](https://github.com/openscriptures/morphhb), available under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). UGNT incorporates the Bunning Heuristic Prototype Greek New Testament from the [Center for New Testament Restoration](https://greekcntr.org/), available under CC BY-SA 4.0. The GOD4.us Greek token identities come from the public-domain Robinson–Pierpont Byzantine Majority Text.

The [pinned source list](../../../../tools/web-alignment-sources.json), [importer](../../../../tools/import-web-alignment.js), and [engineering report](../../../../docs/shell-77-word-study.md) describe the source versions, checksums and reproduction procedure.

Shell 78 adds [ULT v92](https://git.door43.org/unfoldingWord/en_ult/archive/v92.zip) and uses the same UHB and UGNT witnesses. Rows marked `berean-verse` or `berean-sentence` use positional evidence from the Berean Standard Bible third-printing translation tables, produced by Bible Hub, Discovery Bible, OpenBible.com, and the Berean Bible Translation Committee. The publisher makes the tables and Bible available for free use. GOD4.us retained positions supported by the exact WEB reading and the published original-language witnesses, and excluded supplied or incompatible groups. See the [Shell 78 report](../../../../docs/shell-78-word-study.md), [Berean downloads](https://berean.bible/downloads.htm), and [publisher terms](https://berean.bible/terms.htm).

Shell 79 rows marked `dual-phrase` use agreement between the pinned ULT v92 and Berean sources and the same ordered original-language witnesses. The [Shell 79 report](../../../../docs/shell-79-word-study.md) lists the checksums, validation rules and retained groups.

Shell 80 rows use the [Clear.Bible hand-aligned data](https://github.com/Clear-Bible/Alignments/tree/c99bd0ae6946775f932517656308ca19fc706921) by BiblioNexus under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The Hebrew package uses positions from the Westminster Leningrad Codex with morpheme boundaries. Its metadata cites Unicode/XML Leningrad Codex, UXLC 1.8 (26.9), Tanach.us Inc., West Redding, CT, April 2023. The biblical Hebrew text may be copied without restriction,. The Greek package uses the SBL Greek New Testament, edited by Michael W. Holmes and released by the Society of Biblical Literature and Logos Bible Software. The Greek text and the manual Biblica alignment layer declare CC BY 4.0. The target word positions come from the Berean Standard Bible. GOD4.us cross-checked the positions against ULT v92 and the ordered Bible witnesses, projected common groups to exact WEB spans, and published only verified positional agreement. The [Shell 80 report](../../../../docs/shell-80-word-study.md) and [source manifest](../../../../tools/clear-bible-shell80-sources.json) contain the source inventory and checksums. The derived shards remain under CC BY-SA 4.0.
